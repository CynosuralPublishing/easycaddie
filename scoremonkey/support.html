Common questions

How do I start a game?

Choose how many players (1 to 6), type each player's name and pick a color, then tap Start. Anyone you leave unnamed or uncolored gets a name and color automatically.

How do I add or take away points?

Tap a player's score box. Spin the number wheels to the amount, then tap Add or Subtract. The score counts up or down so everyone can see the change.

I entered the wrong score. How do I fix it?

Tap Undo at the top of the screen to take back the last change. Tap Redo to put it back if you undid one too many.

How do I see what happened earlier in the game?

Tap Game Log at the top of the screen. It lists every score change, newest first, with each player's running total.

How do I reset scores or start over?

Tap Menu. Reset Scores sets everyone back to 0 with the same players. New Game takes you back to setup to choose new players.

Did I lose my game?

Your game saves itself after every change. If you close the app, it asks whether you want to continue the next time you open it. Games are stored on your device only, so they can't be recovered after deleting the app.

What information does the app collect?

None. See the privacy policy for details.
