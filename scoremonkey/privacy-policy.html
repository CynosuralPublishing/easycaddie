ScoreMonkey privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

ScoreMonkey ("the app") keeps score for card games, board games and game night. This page explains what the app does with your information.

The short version: ScoreMonkey does not collect, track, or share any of your personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect personal information, use analytics or tracking tools, or show advertising.

What stays on your device

The player names, colors and scores you enter, and the list of score changes in the Game Log, are saved in the app's storage on your phone so you can continue a game later. This information never leaves your device. The app does not upload it, sync it, or share it with anyone.

Starting a new game clears the saved game. Deleting the app removes everything it saved.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, or any other feature of your device. It works without an internet connection.

Third parties

Because the app does not collect your information, there is nothing to share or sell. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics. Apple's App Store and Google Play may provide me with general, anonymous statistics about the app, such as download counts or crash reports from people who choose to share them. Those services are covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the effective date above before the change is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Norm at cynosural.publishing@gmail.com.
