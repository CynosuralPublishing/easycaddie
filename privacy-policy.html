Privacy policy

Easy Caddie and ScoreMonkey · Effective November 25, 2026

This policy covers two apps made by Cynosural Publishing ("the apps"): Easy Caddie, which helps golfers choose a club based on the distance to their target, and ScoreMonkey, which keeps score for card games, board games and game night. This page explains what the apps do with your information.

The short version: neither app collects, tracks, or shares any of your personal information.

What the apps collect

Nothing. The apps have no accounts or sign-in, and they do not collect personal information, use analytics or tracking tools, or show advertising.

What stays on your device

Easy Caddie saves the club names, carry and roll distances you enter, and your settings (yards or meters, and how the app matches clubs), so they are still there the next time you open it.

ScoreMonkey saves the player names, colors and scores you enter, and the list of score changes in the Game Log, so you can continue a game later.

In both apps this information is stored only in the app's storage on your phone or tablet and never leaves your device. The apps do not upload it, sync it, or share it with anyone. You can reset it inside each app at any time, and deleting an app removes everything it saved.

Permissions

The apps do not ask for access to your location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone, or any other feature of your device. They work without an internet connection.

Third parties

Because the apps do not collect your information, there is nothing to share or sell. The apps do not include advertising or third-party analytics. Apple's App Store and Google Play may provide me with general, anonymous statistics about the apps, such as download counts or crash reports from people who choose to share them. Those services are covered by their own privacy policies.

Children

The apps do not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If either app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the effective date above before the change is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Norm at cynosural.publishing@gmail.com.
